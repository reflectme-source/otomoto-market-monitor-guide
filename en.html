First-run workflow

Three steps to recurring monitoring.

STEP 01

Use a public search URL

Copy a filtered OTOMOTO result URL. Choose the maximum pages and number of listings to include.

STEP 02

Create your baseline

The first successful scan records a baseline. Enable “Include current snapshot” to see existing listings immediately; baseline entries are not all new offers.

STEP 03

Schedule and consume changes

Use Apify Schedules for daily or hourly checks. Later scans compare the observed results and emit structured events, which you can export or forward through integrations.

Important details

Frequently asked questions

Will I receive an alert immediately after a price drop?

No. This is scan-based, not real-time monitoring. A change is detected on the next scheduled successful scan; timing depends on your selected schedule and source access.

Does a listing disappearing mean the vehicle sold?

No. An ad can fall out of the monitored result window or become unavailable for other reasons. A result-set disappearance is not proof of sale or deletion.

Can I send the data to Sheets, Make or n8n?

Apify provides datasets, schedules and integrations/webhooks. You can connect them to your workflow; separate configuration on your account may be required.

Does the Actor bypass logins or anti-bot challenges?

No. It reads public search-result pages without bypassing access controls. Site changes and access restrictions can impact completeness.
